A look at the three seas and some of the powerful Blox Fruits in the game.

The Seas

First Sea Levels 1 to 699. Where every player begins.

Second Sea Levels 700 to 1499. where you can start trading your Blox fruits and do raids to awaken your Blox fruit!

Third Sea Levels 1500 to 2800. where you can prove you are the best unlocking the best weapons and hunt other players to claim thier bounty.

Fruits

Dragon A Mythical fruit that lets you transform into a giant dragan.

Tiger A fast Mythical fruit with strong claw attacks and lets you transform into a muscular tiger.
